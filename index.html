Franklin Heng

I'm a CV/ML engineer based in NYC. Building solutions for climate and civic engagement.

  1. 2026 – Present

    Joined Thalo Labs as Senior ML engineer. Building sensors + ML for HVAC.

    Data Diplomat at NYCxDSSG. We provide pro-bono technology support to non-profits in NYC, and host hackathons/workshops/events. Built FOIA Fluent, an open-source civic AI platform for public records.

    I also write on Substack about things I'm interested in like ethical AI, music, and vinyl collecting.

  2. 2025 – 2026

    ML engineer at Make The Dot, building generative AI pipelines for fashion designers. Fine-tuned open-source Stable Diffusion models, experimented with ComfyUI, and did a lot of ML Ops / backend work.

  3. 2022 – 2024

    CV/ML engineer at an ocean-based carbon removal startup.

    • 2D / 3D automated measurement systems for macroalgae.
    • Automated tracking and sinking rate measurement of biomaterials deployed in the ocean.
    • Perception feature for a robotic oyster harvesting platform.
  4. 2020 – 2022

    Co-founded Breathily, in order to make lung function testing more accessible to patients with physical disabilities.

    • Designed and ran an IRB-approved clinical study to test our product on live patients at the UCSF Adult Pulmonary Function Lab.
    • Placed 2nd at UC Launch, funded by UCSF Catalyst and NSF I-Corps, and filed patent.
  5. 2018 – 2020

    CV scientist across three labs, applying computer vision to medical images.

    • Fahy Lab: automated detection and analysis of mucus plugs trapped in lung airways. Published in JCI Insight.
    • Sohn Lab: automated brain age prediction from head CT, using the gap against real age as a systemic disease signal.
    • Abbasi Lab: automated decoding of attentional engagement from 64-channel EEG.
  6. 2016 – 2018

    Received a B.A. in Computer Science. Participated in CS clubs (Codeology and VR Club). Lived in Cloyne Co-op. Summer intern at Samsung, working on mobile GPU development.

    Research at the Video and Image Processing Lab under Prof. Avideh Zakhor on crop stem width phenotyping. Published a paper.

  7. 2015 – 2016

    Where I was first introduced to computer vision.

    Multi-band IR tracking: segmenting and tracking aircraft signatures across shortwave, midwave, and longwave infrared.
