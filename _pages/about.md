I'm an engineer and former EMT based in NYC. I've built solutions across healthcare, climate, and robotics.

I initially chose to study computer science after reading an article on neuroprosthetics. It reminded me of my grandmother, who was half paralyzed after a stroke, and I decided I wanted to build technology that connects with people and society in a positive way.

After college, I worked at UCSF, applying computer vision to [medical images](/projects/10_mucus_plug/) and published a [paper](https://insight.jci.org/articles/view/174124). I later co-founded [Breathily](/projects/09_breathily/), a device that makes lung function testing more accessible for patients with physical disabilities. Afterwards, I was an ML engineer at [Running Tide](https://runningtidexmason.webflow.io/), an ocean-based carbon removal startup, working on [macroalgae phenotyping](/projects/04_kelp_3d/) and [underwater tracking of biomaterials](/projects/06_running_tide_sink/). My last role before moving to NYC was as a full-time EMT in the San Francisco Bay Area, while pursuing firefighting.

Currently, I'm a Data Diplomat at [NYC Data Science for Social Good](https://www.nyc-dssg.org/) and building [FOIA Fluent](https://www.foiafluent.com/), a tool designed to improve government transparency and expand equitable access to reliable public information for the communities that need it most.
