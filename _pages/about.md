I'm a CV/ML engineer based in NYC. I've built solutions across healthcare, climate, and robotics.

I initially chose to study computer science after reading an article on neuroprosthetics. It reminded me of my grandmother, who was half paralyzed after a stroke, and I decided I wanted to build technology that connects with people and society in a positive way.

I was first introduced to computer vision at NASA JPL, where I worked on [automatic segmentation and tracking software](/projects/13_jpl_ir_tracking/). I then did research at the UC Berkeley Video and Image Processing Lab (VIP) and co-authored a [paper](https://library.imaging.org/ei/articles/31/13/art00009) on [agricultural robotics](/projects/08_sorghum/).

After college, I worked at UCSF, applying computer vision to [medical images](/projects/10_mucus_plug/) and published a [paper](https://insight.jci.org/articles/view/174124). I later co-founded [Breathily](/projects/09_breathily/), a device that makes lung function testing more accessible for patients with physical disabilities. Afterwards, I was an ML engineer at [Running Tide](https://runningtidexmason.webflow.io/), an ocean-based carbon removal startup, working on [macroalgae phenotyping](/projects/04_kelp_3d/) and [underwater tracking of biomaterials](/projects/06_running_tide_sink/). Most recently I worked at [Make The Dot](/projects/01_make_the_dot/), building generative AI pipelines for fashion designers.
